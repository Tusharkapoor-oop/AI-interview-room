# Application Flow: Next-Gen AI Interview Room

This document outlines the deeply interactive, premium end-to-end journey for both candidates and recruiters, enriched with advanced Agentic AI features and seamless anti-cheating checkpoints.

## 1. Candidate Journey: The Premium Experience

### Phase 1: The "Digital Lobby", Localization & Onboarding
1. **Secure Access & Region Routing:** Candidate clicks a secure magic link. The global CDN instantly routes them to the nearest Edge Node (e.g., Singapore, Frankfurt, or São Paulo) for sub-50ms latency.
2. **Language & Accent Selection:** The candidate selects their preferred spoken language (e.g., Mandarin, Spanish, English) and their regional dialect.
3. **System Diagnostics (Low-Bandwidth Adaptive):** The 3D interface runs diagnostics. If a slow connection is detected, the platform seamlessly offers a "Low-Bandwidth Mode" that relies on local-device edge computing instead of streaming 4K video.
3. **Identity & Deepfake Verification:**
   - Candidate performs a 3D facial scan (similar to setting up FaceID).
   - A highly engaging "Simon Says" micro-game acts as the liveness check (e.g., "Look at the glowing dot in the top left," "Tap your chin"), seamlessly defeating real-time deepfakes without feeling like an interrogation.
4. **Mock Practice Arena:** The candidate is given 5 minutes in a sandbox environment to chat with the AI, ensuring they are comfortable with the interface before the real assessment begins.

### Phase 2: The Immersive Interview
1. **Dynamic Introduction (Hyper-Polyglot):** The AI (represented by a culturally-appropriate, highly realistic human avatar) greets the candidate natively in their chosen language and seamlessly handles code-switching if the candidate mixes languages.
2. **Behavioral & Gamified Assessment:**
   - The AI asks behavioral questions.
   - *Security Checkpoint:* Acoustic analysis constantly monitors for the 3-4 second delay typical of LLM-generated cheat sheets.
   - The candidate plays a 2-minute neuroscience spatial reasoning game.
3. **Live AI Pair Programming:**
   - A beautifully styled dark-mode IDE slides into view (Monaco Editor integration).
   - The AI presents a real-world architectural problem. As the candidate types, the AI makes live suggestions, asks *why* a specific pattern was chosen, and evaluates code execution.
   - *Security Checkpoint:* Keystroke dynamics monitor for bot behavior. If suspicious activity is detected, a **brief 5-12 second warning popup** appears on the candidate's screen ("Please ensure you are typing naturally") before fading away, minimizing permanent disruption. All underlying telemetry is simultaneously sent to the Admin database.
4. **Agentic Deep Dive:** The AI pulls a specific commit from the candidate's public GitHub and says, *"I noticed you used a recursive approach here last month. How would you optimize this for a high-concurrency environment?"*

### Phase 3: Completion & Instant Feedback
1. **Smooth Wrap-up:** The AI concludes the interview gracefully.
2. **Candidate Dashboard:** The candidate immediately receives access to a personalized dashboard showing non-confidential feedback, such as their communication clarity score and pacing metrics.

## 2. Recruiter Journey: The Command Center

### Phase 1: Agentic Campaign Setup & Localization
1. **One-Click Configuration:** Recruiter pastes a job description. The AI generates requirements and rubrics.
2. **Live Translation Sync:** Recruiter sets their viewing language to English. Even if the candidate interviewed in Japanese, the AI will generate English transcripts, summaries, and sentiment analysis for the recruiter.

### Phase 2: The Intelligence Dashboard
1. **The Grid:** A visually stunning Kanban board of candidates. Each card features a glowing halo indicating the "Trust & Integrity Score" (Green = Authentic, Red = High Risk).
2. **Deep Dive Forensics (Comprehensive Data Store):** Clicking a candidate opens the Command Center.
   - **Centerpiece:** A cinematic video player with a synchronized, interactive transcript.
   - **Biometric 3D Visualizer:** A 3D model representing the candidate's "Cognitive Fingerprint."
   - **Persistent Event Log:** While candidates only saw 5-12 second popups, the Admin sees a **permanent, detailed historical record of every single alert**. The timeline is marked with specific flags: 👁️ *Gaze Anomaly*, ✋ *Device Detected*, 🗣️ *AI Audio Signature Detected*, including exact timestamps, severity levels, and raw sensor data.
3. **Actionable Insights:** The AI provides a 3-bullet summary of *why* a candidate was flagged (e.g., *"Candidate exhibited mechanical reading patterns during the coding phase and had a highly irregular 4-second latency before answering complex questions."*)
4. **ATS Sync:** Recruiter clicks "Advance to Final Round," and all data instantly syncs back to Workday or Greenhouse.
