AI Interview Rooms: Architecture, Threats,
Detection Methods, Limitations, and Advanced
Anti-Cheat Blueprint
Abstract
The rapid adoption of remote hiring has led organizations to deploy Artificial Intelligence (AI)-powered
interview platforms. These systems combine computer vision, speech processing, behavioral analytics, and
identity verification technologies to conduct, monitor , and assess candidates remotely. While AI interview
rooms improve scalability and reduce recruitment costs, they also introduce new challenges related to
cheating, impersonation, deepfakes, privacy, and fairness. This report examines the architecture of modern
AI interview systems, identifies major threats, evaluates current detection techniques, discusses limitations,
and proposes a next-generation anti-cheat framework.
1. Introduction
Traditional hiring processes require physical presence and manual evaluation. Modern organizations
increasingly use AI-driven interview platforms to:
Conduct interviews remotely
Automate initial candidate screening
Assess communication skills
Verify candidate identity
Detect suspicious behavior
Reduce recruitment costs
Handle large applicant volumes
Examples include AI-powered video interviews, coding assessments, technical interviews, and behavioral
evaluations.
The rise of generative AI, voice cloning, and deepfake technology has significantly increased the risk of
interview fraud, making advanced security mechanisms necessary.

10. Conclusion
AI interview rooms represent the future of large-scale remote hiring. However , as AI technologies become
more powerful, cheating techniques are also evolving. No single detection mechanism is sufficient. The
most effective approach combines identity verification, behavioral analytics, gesture recognition, eye
tracking, environmental monitoring, digital security controls, and human review. Future systems must
balance security, fairness, privacy, and accessibility while maintaining trust in remote recruitment
processes.
A defense-in-depth architecture that integrates multiple independent verification layers provides the
highest reliability and resilience against interview fraud.•
